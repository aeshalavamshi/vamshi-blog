### Tip2 : The Sanjeevani for immunity and gut health – Prebiotics, Probiotics and Post-biotics.

#### What are probiotics?

According to the World Health Organization, probiotics are living strains of bacteria (often referred to as “good bacteria”) which, when consumed in adequate amounts, provide health benefits to the person ingesting them.

Since these types of bacteria are only able to take up temporary residence in the gastrointestinal tract (read: days or weeks at maximum), evidence suggests that in order for probiotics to provide an actual positive impact, they must be consumed on a consistent basis—ideally, daily.

#### What are prebiotics?

In contrast to *pro*biotics, which are live bacteria, *pre*biotics are not living organisms—they’re nondigestible food ingredients that strategically support the “good bacteria” that already exist in your gut. Certain prebiotics, such as the ones found in complex carbs, feed the probiotic bacteria so it can grow, while others provide support in different ways (such as by singling out “bad bacteria” to free up space for the good stuff.

Prebiotic fiber is found in vegetables such as parsnips, radishes, raw banana, flax seeds, Garlic and psyllium husk powder.

## PARAMETER 2: IMMUNITY & Inflammation

Inflammation is believed to play a central role in many of the chronic diseases that characterize modern society. Inflammation affects your energy level in three ways.

- First, it causes such life-style diseases and ailments that the quality of life is seriously affected.
- Second, it depletes your body of energy, which it redirects to create the inflammation.
- Third, it makes it more difficult for your body to absorb vital proteins, vitamins, and minerals from food.

What causes chronic inflammation?

Referred from the *<https://www.healthline.com/health/chronic-inflammation#causes>* , Several things can cause chronic inflammation, including:

- untreated causes of acute inflammation, like an infection or injury
- an [autoimmune disorder](https://www.healthline.com/health/autoimmune-disorders), which involves your immune system mistakenly attacking healthy tissue
- long-term exposure to irritants, like industrial chemicals or polluted air

Factors like pollutants and auto-immune disorders are not in our hands. It is determined by where our homes are and what is our genetics. But what is in our hands is protecting the ‘saviour’ bacteria of our guts.
