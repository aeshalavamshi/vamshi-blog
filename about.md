## Why "Circles & Lines"

We each travel along *lines and circles* in our personal lives. Sometimes the circles are virtuous and sometimes vicious. Sometimes we keep finding ourselves at the very point where we started.

But there are also lines, on which we progress from an intention or a desire to the achievement of a goal. Or, just as easily, we deteriorate.

My intention with this blog is to observe, and bring to the fore, the circles and lines that persons, families, institutions, governments and global politics take, as they progress or deteriorate in achieving their objectives.

*(This is the original introduction from my earlier blog, [Facts.and.opinions](https://circlesnlines.wordpress.com/).)*
